How Vandelay works

Vandelay is the app’s AI. Everything it writes carries the Vandelay · AI mark.

What it does

  • Suggests options, and says where the suggestion comes from.
  • Tallies who’s in on what and sets a decision by the rule shown on that screen.
  • Answers questions in chat from what’s on the table.
  • Books everything — only after you tap Book it.

What it never does

  • Vote, or add a preference of its own.
  • Set anything nobody is in on.
  • Charge anyone without a confirmation.
  • Move you to another screen without asking.

What it uses about you

Your answers on the table, where you fly from, and notes you give it in chat (like no shellfish). See and delete them under What Vandelay knows about you.

Changing its mind

Every set decision has Change and Why this one?. Every open one with a deadline has Hold it a day.

In this prototype

Emil, Anna and Dakota are simulated friends, marked with a dashed ring. Vandelay here follows written rules, not a language model. Prices are illustrative.
